Welcome to Kyoto

Kyoto, Japan’s ancient capital, is a city where tradition and modernity blend seamlessly. From ancient temples and shrines to bustling streets and tea houses, Kyoto captivates every visitor.

Rich History

Once the imperial capital of Japan, Kyoto has been the cultural heart of the nation for centuries. Its thousands of temples, shrines, and gardens showcase the evolution of Japanese art and architecture.

Vibrant Culture

Kyoto’s culture thrives through its festivals, kimono artisans, tea ceremonies, and cuisine. It’s a city that honors its past while embracing the present.

Natural Beauty

From the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove to the Philosopher’s Path, Kyoto’s nature offers peace and beauty year-round.
